The Ministry

A people who refuse to be ordinary

Transcendent Life in Christ exists to put the Word of God back at the centre of the believer’s understanding of who they are. Not a people waiting for heaven to begin — a people already carrying the life of God.

When a person is born again, they are not improved. They are born anew. Old things pass away; the fresh and the new has come. What enters the human spirit at that moment is not merely a longer life, but the very life of God — the life that makes God, God.

Most believers have been taught to read eternal life as a promise about duration, something collected later. Scripture speaks of it in the present tense. You already have it. And if the life is already present, then its qualities are meant to be present too: peace where the world offers noise, authority where the world offers fear, and hope that does not depend on the news.

That is the whole burden of this ministry. Not to make Christians busier, but to make them aware. A believer who knows what they are carrying stops living below it.

Definition

transcendent

adjective · from the Latin transcendere

To climb over; to go over, as water goes over the rim of a vessel because it is full. Free from the constraints of the material world.

Kin: supernatural · superior · paranormal · excellent · magnificent

What We Hold

Six convictions, each anchored on a verse

These are not slogans. Each one is a line of Scripture we are prepared to stand on, and each one changes how a believer lives on a Tuesday.

Isaiah 9:6–7

Peace comes before hope

Christ is called the Prince of Peace, which means peace sits at the core of the salvation package. It also means hope has a prerequisite: no one can hope from a heart that has been robbed of peace. Peace is the capacity; hope is what that capacity produces.

1 Timothy 1:1

Hope is a Person

Paul does not describe hope as a mood or a forecast. He names it: the Lord Jesus Christ, which is our hope. Hope is therefore not something you generate on a good day. It is Someone you are joined to on every day.

1 John 5:13

Eternal life is present tense

John writes so that believers may know, with settled and absolute knowledge, that they already have eternal life. It is not a wage paid at the end. It is the life of God, resident now, and meant to be drawn on now.

Colossians 2:15

The victory is already won

The principalities and powers were disarmed and publicly displayed at the cross. The believer is not campaigning for a victory still in doubt; they are enforcing one already secured. You command what has already been defeated.

Hosea 4:6

The real enemy is ignorance

God’s people are destroyed for lack of knowledge — not for lack of power. So the fight the Bible actually assigns us is the good fight of faith, and the ground it is fought on is what we know of the Word.

Matthew 28:18–20

The commission is not optional

The world is asking for a saviour in whatever words it has. Our answer is to go — but you cannot give what you do not have. A hopeless believer has nothing to hand anyone. First be filled, then be sent.

The Apostle

Apostle Benjamin Zulu

Apostle Benjamin Zulu President and Founder

Apostle Benjamin Zulu is the President and Founder of Transcendent Life in Christ. His ministry has one persistent theme: the believer has been handed the life of God, and almost nobody has been told what that means.

He preaches against a Christianity of postponement — the version that files every promise under one day and leaves the believer to survive the week on willpower. In his teaching, the supernatural is not an occasional visitation but the ordinary climate of a person who knows what Scripture says about them.

He is equally direct about where the church’s energy is being wasted. Believers are not losing ground to a devil who still holds authority; they are losing it to what they have never been taught. The correction he offers is not a technique. It is knowledge — applied.

Don’t fight the devil or demons. Fight ignorance. That is what they are using to destroy believers. Fill yourself up with the Word and transcend.
